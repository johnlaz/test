Is the market oversold or overbought?

One check after the close. Market Stretch Terminal reads whether the Nasdaq-100, S&P 500 and Dow are pushed too far down or too far up, and says so in plain words. No account, no API key.

Open the app How it works

One plain reading per index

Each index lands in one of three states. It describes where things stand today. It does not predict.

Oversold

Stretched down

Selling has pushed things far from normal. Bounces get more likely.

Neutral

Nothing stretched

No strong pull either way. Nothing to act on from this tool.

Overbought

Stretched up

Buying has run ahead of itself. Pullbacks get more likely.

How it works

The page isn't live. A scheduled job rebuilds the data after each US close, and the app just reads it.

1

Data after the close

A GitHub Action pulls end-of-day prices and index constituents on weekday evenings.

2

Several signals

Ultimate Oscillator, RSI, breadth (share of stocks above their 50 and 200-day averages), VIX, distance from the peak, and volume.

3

One simple answer

Pick how sensitive it should be (pullback, correction or bear market) and read the status. Past signals show what followed them.

Screening aid only, not investment advice. Data is end-of-day and nothing here is live.
