The call

My recommendation

Be honest with yourself about these:
  • The scores are my editorial ratings built from Donner's published specs and marketing copy, not hands-on listening. Use the "Hear it" buttons and judge the sound yourself.
  • The S200 and J100 are brand new. Independent reviews barely exist yet, so their sound claims rest mostly on Donner's own descriptions.
  • The alternates were picked for size (about 135 cm wide, 35–45 cm deep, under 96 cm tall). Their prices are US retailer listings found today, not your Donner cart; Donner's R300 shows its own site price. Casio's Celviano AP-S450 is another compact option, but I couldn't confirm a current US price, so I left it out.
  • Yamaha, Roland and Kawai scores lean on their published specs and reputation (flagship-grand samples, escapement keys). I haven't heard any of these side by side, so treat sound scores as a starting point.
  • Donner prices are what your cart shows. Each item also lists a promo code (EA10, EA12, EA15) — check the cart total after applying it.
  • Only the S300 in your cart includes a bench. Budget separately for the other five.
  • The J100 is a pre-sale and ships around Nov 14.
  • None of these will sound like a $3,000+ Yamaha or Kawai. They're good home pianos, and for the best sound at night, plug in headphones.
